# Privacy And Analytics

Sacred Book may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, understand which
guides are useful, and spot pages that are slow or difficult to reach.

The analytics beacon is designed without cookies, browser storage, or
cross-site tracking. Cloudflare reports aggregate information such as page
views, referrers, browser and operating-system families, approximate country,
and page-performance measurements. Cloudflare states that the source IP
received during normal request handling is discarded at its nearest data
center rather than stored in its analytics datasets.

The reports are visible only inside the site owner's Cloudflare account. Sacred
Book does not publish an analytics dashboard and does not ask visitors to create
an account.

- [Cloudflare Web Analytics overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/)
- [Cloudflare analytics beacon privacy details](https://developers.cloudflare.com/speed/observatory/rum-beacon/#privacy-information)
